# Bass clarinet

register: 3800–7000
- 3800–5000 low: deep, dark and rich; very soft playing is easy
- 5000–6400 middle: warm, less dark
- 6400–7000 high: clear but thinner
expanded_register: 3400–3800 with a low C extension (most professional instruments have it); 7000–7700 very high: thin, difficult.
transposing: yes: sounds 1400 below written in treble clef (in B♭), 200 below when written in bass clef. Every value here is sounding pitch; write sounding pitch.
clefs: F; G for passages that stay above about 6500
techniques: the woodwind techniques (woodwinds/_family.md), plus:
- wide dynamic range, from silence to loud in the low register
- agile, slower to speak in the lowest notes
- slap tongue: "text": "slap"; strong and percussive in the low register
- key clicks are clearly audible in the low register
- multiphonics: many

## Harmonics
The cylindrical bore overblows at the twelfth and gives only the odd partials.
Partial n sounds at the fingered note + 1200·log2(n): 3 → +1902, 5 → +2786,
7 → +3369, 9 → +3804. From the low fingerings 3800–5000; from 3800: 5702 6586
7169 7604; from 3400 (low C extension): 5302 6186 6769. The real pitch can
differ by a few cents.

Write the sounding pitch with "art": "harmonic".
