# Bassoon

register: 3400–7200
- 3400–4300 low: dark, reedy and powerful; very soft playing is hard below about 3700
- 4300–6000 middle: smooth and versatile, the main register
- 6000–7200 high: tense, plaintive and very expressive
expanded_register: 7200–7700 very high: thin and difficult.
transposing: no
clefs: F; Tenor for passages around 6000–7000
techniques: the woodwind techniques (woodwinds/_family.md), plus:
- crisp, effective staccato, also in the low register
- wide leaps are idiomatic
- bends of about a quarter-tone ("gliss": ±50)
- multiphonics: many

## Harmonics
The conical bore overblows at the octave and gives all partials. Partial n
sounds at the fingered note + 1200·log2(n): 2 → +1200, 3 → +1902, 4 → +2400,
5 → +2786. From the low fingerings 3400–4300; from 3400: 4600 5302 5800 6186.
The real pitch can differ by a few cents. Harmonic (flageolet) fingerings are
also part of the normal technique of the high register.

Write the sounding pitch with "art": "harmonic".
