# Contrabassoon

register: 2200–5000
- 2200–3100 low: the deepest woodwind sound, a buzz rich in partials; slow to speak
- 3100–5000 middle: clearer, the most useful register
expanded_register: 2100 on instruments with a low A key; 5000–5800 high: thin and weak, rarely used.
transposing: yes: sounds 1200 below written. Every value here is sounding pitch; write sounding pitch.
clefs: F8vb
techniques: the woodwind techniques (woodwinds/_family.md), plus:
- slow to speak: fast passages in the low register sound blurred; staccato works at moderate speed
- needs much air: short phrases in the low register
- very soft playing is limited in the lowest notes

## Harmonics
The conical bore overblows at the octave and gives all partials. Partial n
sounds at the fingered note + 1200·log2(n): 2 → +1200, 3 → +1902, 4 → +2400,
5 → +2786. From the low fingerings; from 2200: 3400 4102 4600 4986. The real
pitch can differ by a few cents. Rarely used.

Write the sounding pitch with "art": "harmonic".
