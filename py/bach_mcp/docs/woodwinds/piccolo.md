# Piccolo

register: 7400–10500
- 7400–8100 low: weak and soft, easily covered
- 8100–9600 middle: clear and agile
- 9600–10500 high: brilliant and piercing, carries over the full orchestra
expanded_register: 10500–10800 very high: shrill, loud only. A few piccolos reach down to 7200 with a low C key.
transposing: yes: sounds 1200 above written. Every value here is sounding pitch; write sounding pitch.
clefs: G8va
techniques: the woodwind techniques (woodwinds/_family.md), plus:
- the flute's agility: fast scales, trills, double and triple tonguing
- soft playing is hard in the high register
- bends of about a quarter-tone ("gliss": ±50)

## Harmonics
Overblowing a low fingering gives its partials. Partial n sounds at the
fingered note + 1200·log2(n): 2 → +1200, 3 → +1902, 4 → +2400. From fingerings
7400–8100; from 7400: 8600 9302 9800. The real pitch can differ by a few cents.
Rarely used: they are thin and the normal notes already cover this register.

Write the sounding pitch with "art": "harmonic".
