# Alto flute

register: 5500–8600
- 5500–6700 low: the characteristic register: warm, velvety and soft; easily covered, best with a light texture around it
- 6700–8100 middle: clear, closer to the flute
- 8100–8600 high: thinner and more effortful
expanded_register: 8600–9100 very high: strained, rarely used.
transposing: yes: sounds 500 below written (alto flute in G). Every value here is sounding pitch; write sounding pitch.
clefs: G
techniques: the woodwind techniques (woodwinds/_family.md), plus:
- needs more air than the flute: shorter phrases, especially loud and low
- agile, a little slower than the flute in very fast passages
- bends of about a quarter-tone to a semitone ("gliss": ±50 to ±100)
- whistle tones and jet whistle as on the flute: "text": "whistle tones", "text": "jet whistle"

## Harmonics
Overblowing a low fingering gives its partials. Partial n sounds at the
fingered note + 1200·log2(n): 2 → +1200, 3 → +1902, 4 → +2400, 5 → +2786.
From fingerings 5500–6200; from 5500: 6700 7402 7900 8286. The real pitch can
differ by a few cents. Softer and more veiled than the normal notes.

Write the sounding pitch with "art": "harmonic".
