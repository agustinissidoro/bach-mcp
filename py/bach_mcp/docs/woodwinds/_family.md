# Woodwinds (flutes, oboes, clarinets, bassoons)

One note at a time (except multiphonics), shaped by the breath: a note can
swell and fade while it sounds ("dyn": "p<f>p"). Microtones are played with
special fingerings: write the exact midicents; fast microtonal passages are
harder and the colour changes from note to note.

breath: the player needs silences to breathe. A phrase without a gap can last
about 5–15 s depending on instrument, register and dynamic; leave a gap of at
least 300–500 ms between phrases.

techniques:
- legato (slurred): no slurs in this score; let each note last until the next onset
- tonguing: every separate note is tongued; single tonguing up to about 8 notes per second, double and triple tonguing faster (easiest on the flutes)
- staccato: "art": "staccato"; accent: "art": "accent"; tenuto: "art": "tenuto"
- flutter-tonguing, a rolled r or throat growl: "art": "tremolo" with "text": "flz."
- trill: "art": "trill"
- harmonics: "art": "harmonic" on the sounding pitch (see each instrument's Harmonics)
- multiphonics, several notes from one fingering: a chord of 2–4 notes with "text": "multiphonic"; the pitches depend on the fingering and are approximate; soft to medium dynamics, held notes
- key clicks, the keys struck without blowing: "head": "cross" with "text": "key clicks"; very soft, pitch approximate
- air sound, breath with little or no pitch: "text": "air"
- bends and glissando: "gliss" on the note; how far depends on the instrument
- vibrato on / off: "text": "vib." / "senza vib."
- dynamics: crescendo and diminuendo on one note work in every register
